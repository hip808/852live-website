Privacy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

852 Live LTD is a company registered in the United Kingdom. We are responsible for the personal data described here. You can reach us at info@852live.net.

What we collect

  • Your email address, only if you join our mailing list. We use it to send news about 852 Live shows and tickets. We never sell it or share it for advertising.
  • Messages you send us by email, such as artist or partnership enquiries. We keep them as long as we need to answer and follow up.

How the mailing list works

After you sign up we send a confirmation email. You are added only once you confirm. Every email we send has an unsubscribe link. We keep your address until you unsubscribe. Our legal basis is your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

Who handles data for us

  • Buttondown stores the mailing list and sends our newsletters.
  • Cloudflare hosts this website and forwards our email. Like any host, it sees technical data such as your IP address when you visit.
  • Google supplies the fonts on this site, so your browser contacts Google to load them. Our email inbox is hosted by Gmail.

Some of these providers are based outside the UK. They use recognised safeguards for international transfers.

Cookies and tracking

We do not use advertising cookies or analytics. Your browser stores one small item, your chosen language, on your own device so the site remembers it.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see, correct or delete your data, to object to or restrict how we use it, and to withdraw consent. Email info@852live.net and we will reply within one month. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

We may update this page. The date at the top shows the latest version.
